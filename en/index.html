About me

Hi, I’m Beatriz.

I’m a Spanish teacher for foreigners and I give private lessons online. My job is to help you use the language in the situations that really matter to you: talking with people, understanding what you read and hear, and writing with confidence.

Every student arrives with their own story, their own level and their own reasons for learning Spanish. That’s why I don’t follow a fixed mould: I listen, I prepare each lesson with you in mind, and I adjust as you progress.

What to expect from my lessons

  • Personal attention: the lesson revolves around you, not the other way round.
  • A warm approach and a space where making mistakes is part of learning.
  • Clear goals and a path to reach them, at your own pace.

My method

Learn Spanish your way.

  1. 1

    We get to know each other

    We talk about your level, your goals and what you need Spanish for. That way I know where we’re starting from and where you want to get to.

  2. 2

    We prepare your learning

    We adapt the lessons to your needs: content, pace and types of practice designed for you.

  3. 3

    We move forward together

    We practise, review and adjust. Your progress is steady and you notice it lesson by lesson.

FAQ

What people usually ask me.

Can I start from scratch?

Yes. Whether you’ve never studied Spanish or want to start over, we adapt the lessons to your starting point and move forward step by step.

Which levels can I study?

All of them: from beginner to advanced. In a first conversation we talk about your level and goals to plan the lessons.

Are the lessons one-to-one?

They are private lessons, designed for you and your situation. If you have a different need, for example learning with a relative or a friend, tell me and we’ll talk it through.

Can I prepare for the DELE?

Yes. You can prepare for the DELE with me: we’ll get to know the exam format, practise each part and work on whatever needs it most.

Can I prepare for the SIELE?

Yes. I also offer SIELE preparation, with practice of its tests and attention to its digital format.

How do the online lessons work?

We connect by video call and work live, with content adapted to you. When we get in touch, we agree the practical details together.
